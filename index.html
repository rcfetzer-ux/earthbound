ONETT

a 2.5D homage to EarthBound

WASD or Arrows walk  ·  Shift run
Space or Z talk  ·  walk into a door to go inside
Q E turn the camera  ·  C recentre
T time of day  ·  M mute

All art, music and sound in this project are generated procedurally from code. No assets from the original game are used.
